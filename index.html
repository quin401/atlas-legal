Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The short version: your financial information stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud account if you turn on sync. Atlas has no server for your data and never sees your balances, bills, or transactions. If you choose to allow it, Atlas sends a few anonymous usage events so we can see which features help. You can turn that off anytime.

Information We Collect

Atlas does not collect the financial details you enter: accounts, balances, transactions, bills, debts, goals, or your name. They are never sent to Atlas.

The only information that can leave your device is optional anonymous product analytics, described below. It is off until you allow it.

Anonymous Product Analytics (Optional)

During setup, and anytime in Settings, you can choose whether to share anonymous product analytics. If you allow it, Atlas sends limited events to PostHog, our analytics provider, to help us understand how the app is used. These events include:

  • A random identifier created when the app is installed. It is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID, or device advertising identifier.
  • The app version, and general device details such as device type, iOS version, and language.
  • Milestone events, such as opening the app, saving a paycheck or bill, viewing Safe to Spend, or sharing a referral link.

Analytics never include names, email addresses, amounts, balances, dates, transactions, bill details, or bank information. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell data. You can turn analytics off anytime in Settings, and Atlas stops sending events right away.

Where Your Data Lives

  • On your device. All financial data you enter is stored locally in the app's private storage, protected by standard iOS data protection.
  • In your own iCloud account (optional). If you enable sync, your data is stored in your private CloudKit database, a part of your personal iCloud account that only you can access. Apple provides this infrastructure. Atlas's developer has no access to it and cannot read it.

Sign in with Apple (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Atlas saves the name and email Apple shares with the app in your device's Keychain. It is never sent to Atlas or anyone else. You can sign out in Settings, and Delete All Data removes it.

Profile Photo

If you add a profile photo, it is saved only on your device and is never uploaded.

Face ID & Touch ID

If you enable the app lock, Atlas uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to verify your identity using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. This happens entirely on your device. Atlas never receives, stores, or transmits biometric data.

Notifications

Bill reminders and daily updates are scheduled and delivered on your device using Apple's local notification system. No data is sent to a server to create them.

On-Device Assistant

On supported devices, Atlas can answer questions about your plan using Apple's on-device language model. Your questions and data are processed on your device and are not sent to Atlas.

Sharing With Support

If you ask for help, Settings lets you create a support file with names removed. It is only shared if you choose to send it yourself, for example by email.

Bank Connections

Atlas does not connect to bank accounts. If bank linking is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Advertising

Atlas contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no cross-app tracking.

Deleting Your Data

Use Settings, then Delete All Data, to remove everything Atlas stores on your device, including your profile photo and Sign in with Apple details. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Data synced to iCloud can be managed in your device's iCloud settings. Because analytics are anonymous and not linked to you, we cannot match them to a person. To ask about them anyway, contact us below.

Children's Privacy

Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Atlas's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to atlasfinancialllc@outlook.com.
